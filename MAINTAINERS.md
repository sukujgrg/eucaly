# Maintainers

Release from your Mac with `make release` after updating `VERSION` and committing
and pushing the changes to `main`. The command validates, signs, notarizes, tags,
and publishes, and resumes saved work when retried.

See the [release guide](docs/releasing.md) for setup and recovery, and the
[self-update guide](docs/self-updates.md) for Sparkle signing and verification.
